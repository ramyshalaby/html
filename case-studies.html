Wadico
Mining · Mineral Resources

A complete mining ERP, from the weighbridge to the board room

The challenge

Wadico runs extraction, breaking and drilling through contractors and sells minerals to customers. Contracts, truck weighing, contractor settlements, customer invoicing and management reporting were spread across separate tools and spreadsheets, and the remote weighbridges had an unreliable connection to head office.

What we built

  • A full Odoo ERP implementation covering sales, purchasing, inventory, manufacturing and accounting.
  • MineCore, a mining operations core: contractor contracts for extraction, breaking and drilling, and customer sales contracts, all in one place.
  • Contractor settlements calculated automatically from the confirmed weighbridge tonnage.
  • Customer invoicing from the same confirmed tonnage, so one measured weight drives both contractor payments and sales revenue.
  • Loading orders that open the matching customer contract automatically.
  • Offline-first weighbridges: the scale is read directly in the browser, every station keeps working without a connection and syncs to the central server, and a new station is deployed with one command.
  • Management dashboards: loading orders, sales and an executive board pack.
  • Power BI connected to Odoo for management reporting.
  • Role-based access, including a read-only role for auditors and executives.
SABIC for Export
SABIC for Export
Mining · Drilling & Export

A complete ERP for a mining exporter, from drilling to payroll

The challenge

SABIC for Export was starting operations across drilling, extraction, crushing and export sales, with remote work sites and rotating site crews. Purchasing, contractor contracts and payment certificates, weighbridges, inventory, production, payroll and accounting all had to run in one system from day one, under Egyptian tax and labour rules.

What we built

  • A full Odoo ERP implementation covering purchasing, sales, inventory, manufacturing, HR, payroll and accounting, delivered against an agreed set of business workflows.
  • Purchase-to-pay with a four-level purchase request approval, vendor agreements, and purchase orders checked automatically against the agreement.
  • Contractor contracts with drilling execution: daily drilling reports, wells, and payment certificates with retention, delay penalties and automatic vendor bills.
  • Customer sales contracts and loading orders, with sales orders, deliveries and invoices generated from the weighed quantity.
  • Weighbridge integration: live scale reading, contract matching, duplicate prevention and station-to-server synchronisation.
  • Manufacturing ready for crushing: production orders created from weighbridge movements, with production costing and work-in-progress valuation.
  • Egyptian payroll: income tax, social insurance, medical insurance and the Martyrs’ Fund, with site rotation cycles and partial-month rules.
  • An employee self-service portal: leave, missions, excuses, work from home, loans, payslips and tax certificates, with GPS geofenced check-in at work sites.
  • Biometric attendance with late arrival, early leave, absence and overtime processing.
  • Egyptian chart of accounts, taxes and multi-currency, automatic contractor settlement entries, Egyptian Tax Authority e-invoice document search, and full financial reports.
EQAT Gold Mines
Gold Mining

A complete ERP for a gold mining group, from the mine to the refinery

The challenge

EQAT runs gold production on site, ships to the refinery, imports equipment and supplies, and manages loans and bank transfers across the group’s companies. Production, logistics, purchasing and finance were followed in spreadsheets and by email, so yields, costs and payments were hard to trace.

What we built

  • A full Odoo ERP implementation covering purchasing, inventory, accounting and expenses across the group’s companies.
  • A gold shipment workflow: daily gold bars, shipments and refinery results, with yield and loss calculated automatically and permissions per stage.
  • A logistics hub: shipments with carriers and dates, and import stages tracked on every purchase.
  • Purchase requisitions and automatic replenishment grouped by product category.
  • Long-term service contracts managed on top of purchase agreements.
  • Material consumption charged to cost centers with analytic accounting.
  • Loans with quarterly schedules, a loans dashboard and due-date email alerts.
  • A bank transfer request workflow: request, approval, execution and accounting, in one place.
  • Inventory valuation reviewed against the accounting ledger.
Eltemsah for Iron and Steel
Steel Manufacturing

A complete ERP for a steel plant, from the gate scale to the ledger

The challenge

Eltemsah buys and weighs material at the plant gate, manufactures steel, and sells to a wide customer base. Weighing, contracts, production costs, cheques, petty cash and sales analysis had to come together in one system, with tight control over sensitive accounting actions.

What we built

  • A full Odoo ERP implementation covering sales, purchasing, inventory, manufacturing and accounting.
  • The Captiva weighbridge system, sending every ticket to the company’s central Odoo.
  • An operations core for contracts, weighbridge movements, settlements and invoicing, all driven by the confirmed weight.
  • Manufacturing costs that include the service costs from the bill of materials.
  • Cheque management for incoming and outgoing cheques, with their full lifecycle and accounting.
  • A cash register screen for petty cash and bank.
  • Sales and purchase analysis with returns, net amounts and net margin, and a partner ledger that drills down to product lines.
  • Material consumption charged to cost centers.
  • Sensitive actions such as reset to draft, reversals, returns and cheque deletion restricted by role.
